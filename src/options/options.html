05 / YOUR DATA

Data

Keys, prompts, writing samples, and draft sessions are stored in this Chrome profile. Keys are not synced or exposed to X. Your model provider receives the selected post, instructions, and any context gathered by the agent’s tools. Draft sessions are removed after 24 hours while Chrome is running.

Conversation context may open a temporary background X tab. It reads rendered ancestor posts and quoted text; unavailable or unloaded content cannot be read. You always review and post the final draft yourself.
